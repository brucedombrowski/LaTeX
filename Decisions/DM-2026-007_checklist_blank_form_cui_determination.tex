\dmsection{Purpose}

This memorandum documents the formal determination that the \textbf{blank} Doc 8604 Rev D ``C\&DH / GDS / IT Security Coordination Meeting Checklist'' PDF form is \textbf{not Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI)} and may be distributed without CUI markings or handling restrictions. This determination applies only to the blank, unfilled form as shipped from the source repository.

\dmsection{Background}

Doc 8604 Rev D is a LaTeX-generated AcroForm PDF checklist used during the ISS Payload C\&DH / GDS / IT Security Coordination Meeting. It contains 485 fillable form fields across 14 sections covering Legacy C\&DH, Onboard Ethernet, KuIP Services, NTP, Anti-Virus, SSC Change Management, NAS Services, JSL, Visiting Vehicles, HOSC GDS, and HRDL.

When a Payload Developer (PD) fills the form with payload-specific data (MAC addresses, IP addresses, KuIP identifiers, IT security configurations), the \textbf{filled} copy becomes CUI under the Export Controlled category (CUI//SP-EXPT//FEDCON). This distinction --- blank vs.\ filled --- drives different handling requirements.

A formal compliance assessment was conducted against authoritative sources (see Section~6) and is documented in \texttt{docs/compliance/} of the project repository.

\dmsection{Applicable Standard}

\textbf{32 CFR Part 2002.4} defines Controlled Unclassified Information as:

\begin{quote}
\textit{``information the Government creates or possesses, or that an entity creates or possesses for or on behalf of the Government, that a law, regulation, or Government-wide policy requires or permits an agency to handle using safeguarding or dissemination controls.''}
\end{quote}

Source: 32 CFR 2002.4, via Cornell LII (\url{https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/32/part-2002}), verified April 10, 2026.

\dmsection{Analysis}

The blank Doc 8604 Rev D PDF contains the following categories of content:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Section headings} (e.g., ``Section 5: Ku Band IP Services'') --- publicly citable SSP document structure
    \item \textbf{SSP document numbers and paragraph references} (SSP 57000 I.3.5, SSP 52050 3.3.7, etc.) --- publicly available document identifiers
    \item \textbf{Empty AcroForm fields} (text fields, checkboxes) --- structure, not data
    \item \textbf{KuIP protocol names} (ICMP, SSH, FTPS, RDP, etc.) --- IETF-standard protocol names, publicly known
    \item \textbf{``How to use'' instructions and CUI Designation checkboxes} --- procedural guidance
    \item \textbf{Applicable Requirements callouts} (per-section SSP paragraph citations) --- references to the requirement, not the requirement text itself
    \item \textbf{DocuSign-style signature and initial placeholder boxes} --- empty visual elements
\end{itemize}

Each category was tested against the CUI Registry definitions for:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{SP-EXPT (Export Controlled):} No export-controlled technical data is present in the blank form. The form captures payload-specific technical data only when filled.
    \item \textbf{ISVI (Information Systems Vulnerability Information):} The blank form describes the \textit{structure} of ISS C\&DH interfaces but does not reveal specific vulnerabilities, configurations, IP addresses, or attack surfaces. SSP document numbers are publicly citable.
    \item \textbf{SP-CTI (Controlled Technical Information):} No technical data with military or space application beyond publicly available SSP paragraph numbers.
    \item \textbf{SP-PROPIN / PRVCY:} No proprietary business information or personally identifiable information.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Conclusion:} No content in the blank PDF triggers any CUI Registry category. The blank form does not meet the 32 CFR 2002.4 definition of CUI.

\dmsection{Decision}

\textbf{The blank Doc 8604 Rev D checklist form is NOT CUI.}

\begin{itemize}
    \item The blank PDF may be distributed without CUI markings
    \item The blank PDF may be committed to private repositories accessible to authorized users
    \item The blank PDF may be emailed without encryption (though encryption is still recommended as good practice)
    \item The blank PDF carries the notice ``THIS FORM CONTAINS CUI WHEN FILLED IN'' per the NARA-recognized pattern for forms that become CUI upon completion
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Once any payload-specific data is entered}, the filled copy is \textbf{CUI//SP-EXPT//FEDCON} and must be:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Stamped with compliant CUI banner via \texttt{tools/mark\_cui.py}
    \item Distributed only via NASA ORBIT Files (preferred) or encrypted email
    \item Handled per 32 CFR Part 2002 and NPR 2810.7
\end{itemize}
